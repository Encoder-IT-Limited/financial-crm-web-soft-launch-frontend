Corporate Tax
UAE Corporate Tax Law · 9% on net profit above AED 375,000 · effective June 2023
🏛
UAE Corporate Tax — Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022
Applicable to all UAE businesses. 9% tax rate on taxable income exceeding AED 375,000 per financial year. Income below AED 375,000 is taxed at 0%. Free zone qualifying income may be eligible for 0% rate subject to conditions. Tax must be filed with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA).
